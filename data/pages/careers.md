#### The Role

The Product Manager will drive the strategic direction and successful delivery of products, ensuring alignment with business objectives and market needs. This role focuses on overseeing the entire product lifecycle, from conceptualization and feature management to performance assessment and solutions delivery, with a particular emphasis on leveraging AI technologies.

The Product Manager will be responsible for coordinating closely with engineering teams and various stakeholders to translate market demands into tangible product features. The ideal candidate is a highly communicative and experienced product professional with a strong background in UI/UX and a deep understanding of artificial intelligence, particularly LLMs.

At Stratpoint, we enjoy the autonomy, lack of bureaucracy, and the freedom to experiment but without the chaos. We're like a startup but with adult supervision.

You will be supported by capable management of HR, Finance and IT that adheres to the highest standards of integrity and good governance.

- Wellness programs such as Wellness Wednesdays
- HMO coverage
- Team buildings and quarterly events (company outings and festive celebrations for Chinese New Year, Valentines, Halloween and Christmas)
- And, friendly intramural competitions

Your TeamIn this role, you will be:

- Directly reporting to the AI Delivery Manager
- Includes client communication with Client PO and other stakeholders for Requirements Gathering
- You will work closely with our business partners, the household brands in technology, such as Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, Microsoft, and our internal AI Engineering team.

#### Your Responsibilities

- Manage the entire product feature lifecycle from conception through launch and post-launch iteration, ensuring alignment with overall product strategy and business objectives.
- Define key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics to assess product performance, regularly monitoring and analyzing data to identify areas for improvement and growth.
- Oversee the successful delivery of product solutions, ensuring they meet defined requirements, quality standards, and are launched effectively to market.
- Collaborate closely with the Engineering Team to translate product requirements into technical specifications, facilitate development, and ensure timely and high-quality implementation.
- Coordinate with cross-functional stakeholders, including sales, marketing, and customer support, to gather requirements, communicate product updates, and ensure alignment across the organization.
- Conduct market research and competitive analysis to identify new opportunities, understand user needs, and inform product roadmap decisions, particularly concerning Large Language Models (LLMs) and other areas of Artificial Intelligence.
- Prioritize product backlog items based on business value, technical feasibility, and strategic importance, ensuring a clear and actionable roadmap for development.
- Communicate product vision, strategy, and roadmap effectively to all internal and external stakeholders, fostering transparency and alignment.

#### About You

- Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering, Business Management, or a related field
- 1-3 years of experience in project coordination, business analysis, or software/AI delivery within an IT or technology environment
- Strong communication skills and good interpersonal and facilitation skills
- Experience in Agile/Waterfall project management principles
- Experience gathering and documenting requirements, and/or coordinating or managing project timelines and deliverables
- Experience with tools such as MS Project, Asana, JIRA, Trello, Smartsheet, Draw.io, Figma, or FigJam
- Ability to organize complex ideas into structured documents or presentation decks and communicate clearly to stakeholders
- Analytical and inquisitive mindset with strong attention to detail
- Ability to work independently, prioritize under pressure, and stay self-motivated against deadlines
- Product management experience
- Systems analysis and design skills
- Experience in U/UX is a plus
- Familiarity with LLMs and other areas of AI

- Vacation and Sick leave
- Wellness programs such as Wellness Wednesdays
- HMO coverage
- Team buildings and quarterly events (company outings and festive celebrations for Chinese New Year, Valentines, Halloween and Christmas)
- And, friendly team competitions

#### Your Team

You will collaborate directly with the Marketing Manager and act as a creative mentor to the Brand & Innovation team. You'll be a key partner for brand direction, communications, and cross-functional visual strategy.

#### Your responsibilities

- Deliver all creative output types  -  static, motion, brand collateral, campaign assets  -  at an expert level
- Set visual identity direction in collaboration with the Marketing Manager (this role contributes to and helps execute brand direction; it does not own brand identity end-to-end)
- Mentor other designers on craft, technique, and creative quality
- Establish the end-to-end delivery process for the design pipeline
- Provide visual/creative input on account-based marketing (ABM) materials in partnership with the ABM team, where shared design resourcing applies
- Consistently determines the root cause of creative/production problems and works with others to resolve them
- Integrates ideas across disciplines to develop original solutions, not just executing a brief literally
- Tailors communication approach to the audience  -  comfortable presenting creative rationale to both the design team and non-design stakeholders
- Comfortable managing change  -  new tools, formats, or brand direction  -  without needing heavy oversight

#### About You

- 3-5 years in graphic design or a related creative role, with a portfolio showing range across static, motion, and brand-collateral work
- Demonstrated experience contributing to or helping maintain a brand's visual identity system, even if not formally owning it
- Some experience informally mentoring junior designers is a plus
- Expert-level proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (or equivalent)
- Practical, workflow-level experience with AI-assisted design tools (e.g., Adobe Firefly, Canva AI)
- Video production and photography skills, including planning and leading shoots
- Vendor and production budget management  -  basic literacy managing external vendors or production costs
- Basic campaign-performance literacy  -  comfort understanding how creative choices connect to KPIs/ROI, even without owning those numbers directly

#### Nice to Have

- Experience supporting account-based marketing (ABM) campaigns with tailored visual assets (pitch decks, account-specific creative)
- Familiarity with brand identity design principles, even without formally holding a brand-strategy role

- Strong experience with AI/ML frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, or similar.
- Hands-on experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure), including deploying and managing AI/ML services.
- Experience with agent orchestration frameworks (e.g., LangChain, LangGraph) and developing multi-agent systems is highly desirable.
- Experience in predictive modeling use cases (e.g., demographic segmentation, forecasting, cost optimization) and frameworks such as scikit-learn, NumPy, Pandas, Optuna.
- Familiarity with data governance (including frameworks for data quality checks, lineage, security, and compliance) is a plus.
- Familiarity with data engineering principles and tools (e.g., Kafka, Spark) for building robust AI pipelines.
- Excellent problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate complex business problems into technical AI solutions.
- Exceptional communication, presentation, and client-facing skills, with the ability to articulate technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
- Ability to work independently, lead projects, and collaborate effectively within a cross-functional team.
