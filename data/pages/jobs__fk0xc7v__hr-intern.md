## Role Overview & Details

As an HR Intern at Stratpoint, you will immerse yourself in the essential frameworks that drive our HR team. Your role will be crucial in generating and refining innovative ideas, deepening your understanding of our practices to drive continuous improvement and innovation.

#### Who We Are

We are trusted, modern technology leaders in:

- Agile Software Development
- Quality Assurance
- Cloud Consulting and Managed Services
- Data Engineering and Analytics
- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

We use technology to enrich experiences, empower businesses, and uplift communities.

Our C.A.R.E. values:

- nourish Creativity
- embody Agility
- manifest Reliability
- pursue Evolution.

#### The Benefits

- Internship Allowance will be provided
- Team buildings and quarterly events (office celebrations for Chinese New Year, Valentines, Halloween and Christmas)

#### Your Responsibilities:

- Provide assistance in different facets of HR
- Prepare HR-related reports and trackers
- Participate in organizing company events
- Other HR-related duties that may be assigned

#### About YOU

- Currently pursuing a degree in Human Resources, Psychology, Business, or any related field.
- Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal.
- Creativity in developing engaging publicity materials.
- Proficiency in using Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Zoom, Canva.
- Ability to learn quickly and adapt to new tools and processes.
- Strong organizational and multitasking abilities.
- Can report onsite, as needed.
- Can report in Boni, Mandaluyong for twice to thrice a week
- Can commit to work for a minimum of 400 hours
