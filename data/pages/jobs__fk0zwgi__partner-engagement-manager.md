#### The Role

Are you the type of person who naturally connects dots, builds bridges, and genuinely loves turning contacts into lasting professional relationships?

Stratpoint is looking for a dynamic Partner Engagement Specialist/Manager to fuel our growth engine. In this role, you won't just sit behind a spreadsheet; you will be the face, voice, and energy driving our strategic relationships with global tech giants, resellers, and distributors. You will act as the ultimate liaison - managing partner programs, running co-marketing events, and keeping our deal pipeline buzzing across our business ecosystems.

#### About You / Candidate Profile

We are looking for someone with 2 to 3 years of experience in B2B tech sales, marketing, or account management who brings a distinct energy to the team. You are a perfect fit if you are:

- Proactive & Dynamic: A high-energy individual who drives collaboration, commands engagement, and excites partners to work with the company.
- A Genuine People Person: You love human connection, thrive on communication, and build authentic, long-term professional rapport.
- Professionally Persistent: You possess the natural drive to consistently follow up, track deals, and maintain partner momentum smoothly without crossing lines.
- Deeply Curious: You have a hunger to learn. Tech moves fast - you don't need to be a coder, but you want to understand how Cloud, AI, Data, and QA solve real business problems, and you ask the right questions to understand what makes a partner's business tick.

#### Key Qualifications

- Bachelor's Degree in Business, Marketing, Communications, IT, or any related field.
- 2-3 years of proven experience in Partner Management, Tech Account Management, or B2B Sales/Marketing within the IT services space.
- Full professional English fluency with exceptional presentation and communication skills.
- Well-organized and comfortable with tools like MS Office, Google Workspace, Zoom, and basic CRM/pipeline tracking.
- Foundational understanding of the IT landscape (it's a massive plus if you already understand what AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, or enterprise software ecosystems do).

#### Your Responsibilities

- Nurture the Ecosystem: Build, develop, and maintain deep relationships with our technology partners, serving as their primary, go-to point of contact.
- Own the Pipeline: Manage and qualify partner-led leads, report on deal statuses, and make sure our sales team is proactively capitalizing on partner opportunities.
- Maximize Partner Perks: Ensure Stratpoint stays compliant and fully optimized with partner programs. You'll handle funding requests (MAP/POC), monitor awards/credits, and coordinate required team certifications.
- Create Buzz: Collaborate with internal marketing and business units to execute fun, impactful co-marketing activities like webinars, podcasts, tech events, and email campaigns.
- Strategize & Align: Help create joint Go-To-Market (GTM) plans that align our services with partner capabilities to drive mutual revenue and growth targets.
- Collaborate Internally: Work closely with sales, marketing, business heads, and technical leads to ensure successful partnerships and oversee deals to ensure opportunities are progressing smoothly.

#### Nice to Have:

- Knowledge on most  Business Units (QA, AI, SS, Cloud, Data)
